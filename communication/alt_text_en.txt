Editorial map of Brazil using 2024 IBGE data. Mato Grosso is outlined and labelled MT. It accounts for 26.6% of Brazil’s planted area in soybeans, grain corn, sugar cane and herbaceous cotton, compared with 6.3% of agricultural machinery rental establishments without operator. Mato Grosso has 267 such establishments and 21.17 million planted hectares across the four crops. Map colours show crop-area share minus establishment share: copper for a higher crop share and teal for a higher rental share. This descriptive contrast does not prove unmet demand. Planted hectares can count the same land repeatedly, and rental serves other agricultural uses. Sources: IBGE CEMPRE table 9528, CNAE 77.31-4, and PAM table 1612.
